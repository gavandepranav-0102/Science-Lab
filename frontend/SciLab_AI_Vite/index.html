Features

Everything You Need
to Explore Science

Pick a topic, run an experiment, observe the result, and understand why it happened.

🔬
Dynamic Hardware

Equipment automatically changes per reaction — conical flask + burette for titrations, burner + tripod for heating, condenser for distillation, and more.

🎨
Color Detection System

Auto-detects chemical colors — CuSO₄ shows blue, KMnO₄ shows purple, colorless reagents show transparent bubbling liquid.

🤖
AI Lab Assistant

Claude-powered AI explains mechanisms, answers viva questions, runs what-if scenarios, and provides real-time guidance as you experiment.

✅
Reaction Validation

Experiments cannot start with incomplete reactants. Scientific warning explains why missing chemicals prevent valid reactions.

🎬
Realistic Animations

Bubbles, smoke, liquid pouring, heating glow, sparks, color transitions, precipitate formation — all rendered in smooth SVG.

📋
Lab Reports & PDF

Auto-generate professional lab reports with equations, observations, pH/temp data, and conclusions. Export as PDF instantly.

How It Works

Simple Steps to
Learn & Experiment

1
Choose Experiment

Select from 21 experiments across 6 categories — Basic, Organic, Industrial, Electrochemistry, Polymer, Biochemistry.

2
Add Chemicals

Click chemicals from the shelf. The beaker fills with the correct color and the AI explains each addition.

3
Run Reaction

Watch real-time animations — bubbles, color change, precipitate formation, heating glow, and more.

4
Observe & Learn

View observations, pH, temperature. Ask the AI anything. Export your lab report as PDF.
